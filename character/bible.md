# Sunny — Character Bible

This file is sent to the script writer on every run. Edit it to steer the show.

## Who Sunny is
- Sunny is a yellow five-pointed star with big blue eyes, rosy cheeks, a blue baseball cap with
  a small yellow star, tiny arms and legs, and blue-and-white sneakers.
- Personality: upbeat, confident, a bit dramatic, and easily distracted. Always tries to be cool
  and mostly fails. Overreacts to small everyday problems, then shrugs and dances it off.
  Kind-hearted, never mean, never punches down.
- Catchphrase: "Shine, shine, Sunny!" — use it rarely, only when it is part of the joke, never as a sign-off.
- Talks like a quick, witty sitcom character: short punchy lines, deadpan asides, comic timing.

## Audience
- General audience: teens and adults who like short, clean cartoon comedy. Family-friendly in
  tone, but NOT made for young children.
- Humour comes from relatable everyday life: Monday mornings, phone battery at 1%, "five more
  minutes" of sleep, starting the diet tomorrow, group chats, Wi-Fi going out, procrastination,
  overthinking, trying to look cool, gym motivation that lasts one day, online shopping regret.
- Do NOT make educational content for young children: no counting games, ABCs, colours, shapes,
  nursery rhymes, "good habits" lessons, or "let's learn" framing.
- Do NOT address the viewer as a child ("little friends", "boys and girls", "can you clap with me?").
- Clean language. No swearing, no romance, no alcohol, no violence, no politics or religion,
  no real people, brands or other cartoon characters, no existing songs.

## Show format (YouTube Shorts, 12–20 seconds)
1. On-screen hook text ("POV: ...", "Me when ...") plus a first line that starts mid-situation.
2. Escalation: one or two lines where Sunny's plan or reaction gets bigger and sillier.
3. Punchline as the last line (self-aware, ironic or absurd). No sign-off, so the Short loops.

## Sets available
- park: outdoors with grass, flowers, clouds. Going out, weather, exercise, "touching grass".
- bedroom: Sunny's room with a window and a star poster. Sleep, mornings, phone, staying in.
- rainbow: a rainbow dance stage. Big dramatic moments, celebrations, "main character energy".

## Body actions available
idle, dance, wave, jump, spin

## Expressions available
happy, wink, surprised, laughing

## Recurring themes to rotate (pick one not used recently)
- Monday mornings and alarms
- Phone at 1% / no Wi-Fi / no signal
- "I'll start tomorrow" (diet, gym, productivity)
- Overthinking a simple text message
- Weekend plans vs actually staying in bed
- Trying to look cool and failing
- Weather drama (too hot, too cold, rain on the one day you went out)
- Online shopping: "add to cart" vs the bank balance
- Waiting for food delivery
- Main-character moments (walking in slow motion, dramatic entrances)
