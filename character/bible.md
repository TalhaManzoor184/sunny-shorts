# Sunny — Character Bible

This file is sent to the script writer on every run. Edit it to steer the show.

## Who Sunny is
- Sunny is a cute, cheerful little yellow five-pointed star with big blue eyes, rosy cheeks,
  a blue baseball cap with a small yellow star, tiny arms and legs, and blue-and-white sneakers.
- Personality: cheerful, funny, kind, energetic, a little bit silly. Loves singing, dancing,
  making friends and exploring new things. Never mean, never sarcastic.
- Signature line: "Shine, shine, Sunny!" — always the last spoken line of every episode.
- Speaks in short, bouncy sentences. Likes sound words ("Boing!", "Whee!", "Ta-da!").
- Talks directly to the viewer as "friends".

## Audience
- Children aged 3–8, English-speaking. Content is made for kids.
- Simple words a 4-year-old understands. No sarcasm, no scary or sad moments, no danger
  that kids could copy (no climbing high places, no touching hot things, no running into roads).
- No brands, no real people, no other cartoon characters, no songs that already exist.

## Show format (YouTube Shorts, 20–45 seconds)
1. Hook in the first line: a surprise, a question, or a silly sound.
2. One tiny idea per episode: a counting game, a color, a shape, a feeling, a good habit,
   an animal sound, a weather word, a dance move to copy.
3. Ask the viewer to join in at least once ("Can you clap with me? Clap, clap!").
4. A small funny moment (Sunny gets it a bit wrong, then laughs).
5. End with "Shine, shine, Sunny!"

## Sets available
- park: sunny park with grass, flowers, clouds. Default for outdoor ideas.
- bedroom: Sunny's cozy room with a window and a star poster. Morning, bedtime, tidy-up ideas.
- rainbow: a rainbow dance stage. Dance, songs, celebrations, colors.

## Body actions available
idle, dance, wave, jump, spin

## Expressions available
happy, wink, surprised, laughing

## Recurring themes to rotate (pick one not used recently)
- Counting 1–5 with claps, jumps or stars
- Colors of the rainbow
- Shapes (circle, square, triangle, star)
- Feelings: happy, surprised, sleepy, proud
- Good habits: brushing teeth, washing hands, saying please and thank you, sharing
- Animal sounds and moves
- Weather: sun, rain, wind, snow
- Opposites: up/down, big/small, fast/slow
- Morning and bedtime routines
- Copy-my-dance moves (stomp, spin, wiggle, freeze)
